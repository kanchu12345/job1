Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

For Businesses Seeking Investment:

How do I submit my business?

Simply fill out the "Submit Listing" form with your business details, required investment amount, and upload high-quality images. Once submitted, your listing will be reviewed by our team.

How long does the approval process take?

Your listing will initially be marked as "Pending." Our admin team typically reviews submissions within 24-48 hours. If approved, it will immediately be "Published" to the public directory.

What are the "Optional Professional Services"?

To help you attract investors, we offer expert assistance in Business Registration, Business Plan/Pitch Deck creation, Business Valuation, and Tax Advisory. You can request these during submission or from your dashboard.

For Investors:

How do I find a business to invest in?

Use our Search & Filter tools to narrow down opportunities by Industry (e.g., Agriculture, Tech), Investment Range (LKR), or specific Location (District/Province).

How do I contact a business?

Click "View Details" on any listing card to see the full pitch, required investment, and direct contact details (Email/Phone) of the business owner.
